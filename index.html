About

A decade
behind the brush.

HTA is a bridal and party makeup artist working across the UK and beyond. What began as a fascination with how makeup can quietly transform confidence has become a craft built on listening first, applying second.

PORTRAIT — the artist

The philosophy

I believe makeup should look like the best, most rested version of you — not a mask, not a trend you'll wince at in ten years. Every appointment starts with your face, your skin and a proper conversation about how you want to feel.

From there it is all craft: skin prep that lasts, colour chosen for your undertone, and a finish built to survive tears, dancing and a long day in front of the camera.
